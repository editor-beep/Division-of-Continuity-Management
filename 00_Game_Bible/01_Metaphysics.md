THE DIVISION OF CONTINUITY MANAGEMENT Metaphysical Rulebook (Internal Use Only — Processor Clearance Θ-3 and above)
Foundational Axiom
“That which is Named, Measured, and Filed becomes Real. That which is Unfiled unravels.”
Reality is not a fixed substrate. It is a vast, decaying ledger maintained through continuous bureaucratic ritual. The universe is administrative in nature: an infinite filing cabinet that requires constant human labor to prevent total ontological collapse (the “Unraveling”).
All existence is composed of Continuum Threads — strands of meaning, memory, identity, and symbolic charge that bind people, places, events, and concepts together. Without proper classification and maintenance, these threads fray, tangle, or dissolve.
Core Metaphysical Principles
	1	The Primacy of Taxonomy To exist is to be categorized. An uncategorized thing exerts “entropic drag” on surrounding reality.
	◦	Naming something is an act of ontological surgery.
	◦	Re-naming something is an act of violence or mercy (often both).
	◦	Contradictory or overlapping categories create “knots” that manifest as anomalies, ghosts, miracles, or economic miracles.
	2	Symbolic Economy Everything has measurable Mythic-Economic Value (MEV).
	◦	Grief can be converted into artistic fuel, national stability, or pharmaceutical calm.
	◦	Childhood wonder can be refined into innovation credits or nostalgia commodities.
	◦	Rebellion contains high yields of raw revolutionary energy but is dangerously unstable. Processors like you perform alchemical labor: refining messy human raw material into usable metaphysical currency that keeps the world stable.
	3	Memory as Infrastructure Personal memories are not private. They are load-bearing structural elements of consensus reality.
	◦	Forgetting is compression.
	◦	Repressing is reinforcement.
	◦	Reclassifying is literal rewriting of local spacetime. Mass reclassification events (e.g., national trauma reassignment campaigns) can literally change history, geography, or the color of the sky.
	4	The Continuity Gradient Reality exists on a spectrum from Hyper-Real (over-documented, rigid, sacred) to Unraveling (undocumented, fluid, dangerous). The Division’s job is to keep most of humanity in the stable middle band. Too much coherence creates brittle authoritarian stasis. Too little creates chaotic dissolution.
	5	The Processor’s Paradox The more skillfully you maintain the system, the more the system rewrites you to better serve it. Your own Continuity File is both your greatest tool and the noose. Every efficient processing session increases your Dissolution Index while raising your Clearance Level.
The Great Ledger
All of existence is mirrored in the Akashic Mainframe — an immaterial but very real database. Every soul has a master file. Every file contains nested sub-files (childhood, relationships, sins, potential futures).
Key Fields in Every File (visible to varying Processor levels):
	•	Narrative Stability (how well your story holds together)
	•	Symbolic Charge (MEV potential)
	•	Inherited Debt (ancestral patterns)
	•	Emotional Surplus/Deficit
	•	Mythic Residue (how much archetypal weight you carry)
	•	Continuity Anchor Points (memories/people/places keeping you from floating away)
The Divisions (Major Departments)
	•	Continuity Management (Player’s Division): Day-to-day maintenance of individual and small-scale threads.
	•	Erasure & Redaction: Professional forgetting. The kinder face of death.
	•	Mythic Commodities: Harvesting and branding archetypes (the Hero, the Martyr, the Mad Artist, etc.).
	•	Narrative Compliance: Ensuring personal stories align with approved meta-narratives.
	•	Emotional Surplus Recycling: Turning despair, longing, and rage into usable energy.
	•	Taxonomic Integrity: Preventing category collapse (the most dangerous and prestigious work).
	•	The Archive of Unraveling Cases: Where failed or exemplary files go. Some are studied. Some are worshipped.
Metaphysical Laws in Practice (Game Rules)
	•	Decision Resonance: Every form you complete sends ripples. A single reclassification of one person’s grief can strengthen or weaken an entire city’s emotional infrastructure.
	•	Echoing: Process a version of yourself enough times and you begin encountering “echo files” — alternate-timeline versions of you in the queue.
	•	Category Collapse: If too many contradictory labels are applied to one entity, they become a “Singularity Case” — capable of rewriting local reality (extremely high risk/reward).
	•	The Law of Conservation of Meaning: You cannot destroy meaning, only redistribute it. “Erasing” a traumatic memory simply moves that pain into someone else’s file or into the collective unconscious.
	•	Player Dissolution Curve: Stats slowly shift based on playstyle:
	◦	High Efficiency → High Dissolution, machine-like clarity, loss of personal identity.
	◦	High Empathy → System strain, risk of Unraveling events, possible rebellion path.
	◦	High Creativity → Chaotic but powerful reality edits, danger of becoming an anomaly yourself.

	•	The final promotion is to become part of the taxonomy itself: an eternal administrative principle (“The Processor,” “The Witness,” “The Last Human,” etc.).
