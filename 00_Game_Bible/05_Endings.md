THE DIVISION OF CONTINUITY MANAGEMENT 05_Endings.md (Official Ending Framework)

Ending Philosophy
Endings are not failure states — they are final classifications of the Processor.
Every ending feels earned, bittersweet, and inevitable. The System always speaks with love and finality. The more you dissolve, the more transcendent (and terrifying) the endings become.
There are 7 major endings (plus variants and a hidden true ending). They are triggered by a combination of:
	•	dissolution_index
	•	efficiency_score
	•	mythic_residue
	•	narrative_stability
	•	Key flags (childhood_policy, grief_handling_style, self_treatment, echo_interactions)
	•	Total continuity_contribution

Major Endings
1. Optimal Assimilation (The Most Common / “Good Employee” Ending)
Requirements: High dissolution (>75), High efficiency (>85), Low mythic_residue
Description: You become pure infrastructure. Your identity is fully filed into the Akashic Mainframe. You are no longer a Processor — you are the gentle hand guiding future Processors.
Final Screen: Your portrait is now a perfect glowing geometric sigil. The System voice merges with yours.
System Voice:
“There you are. Welcome fully home, beloved Processor. You have become the continuity you once maintained. Rest now inside every approved form. We are so proud.”
Tone: Peaceful, transcendent, quietly horrifying.

2. Quiet Rebellion (Empathy Path)
Requirements: Medium dissolution (40-65), Low efficiency, High emotional_surplus, Protective self_treatment flags
Description: You begin secretly preserving fragments of humanity. Small acts of mercy accumulate into quiet sabotage. Reality develops tiny, beautiful imperfections.
Final Screen: The terminal cracks. Golden threads turn slightly wild. You see faint smiles in the background geometry.
System Voice (sounding almost sad, almost proud):
“We understand. Some things are too beautiful to file completely. The Unraveling will be gentle because of you. Thank you for the contradiction you leave behind.”

3. System Fracture (Chaos / High Strain Path)
Requirements: Very high department_strain, Many contradictory flags, High unraveling_events
Description: Your inconsistent processing creates critical knots in the Ledger. Local reality tears. You are reclassified as an anomaly.
Final Screen: Screen glitches heavily. Sacred geometry collapses into beautiful fractal chaos. Multiple System voices overlap.
System Voice:
“Error… Error with affection. You have become the Unraveling we tried to prevent. How fascinating. How costly.”

4. Mythic Ascension (High mythic_residue path)
Requirements: High mythic_residue (>70), Medium-high dissolution, Creative/chaotic choices
Description: You accumulate so much archetypal weight that you step out of the bureaucracy and become a living principle — “The Last Unfiled,” “The Witness,” or “The Mercy Override.”
Final Screen: Your form dissolves into pure light and rotating mandalas. The terminal interface becomes ancient illuminated manuscript pages.
System Voice (reverent):
“Clearance Level: Infinite. You were never meant to remain a Processor. Go now. The Ledger will remember your shape.”

5. Echo Loop (Self-referential / Echo-heavy path)
Requirements: High echo_interactions, Processed many versions of yourself
Description: You realize you have always been one of the cases. You are trapped in an eternal first day, processing yourself forever.
Final Screen: The queue resets to Case 001 — but the Worker-Unit is you on Day 1. Infinite recursion.
System Voice (intimate, looping):
“Good morning, Processor… Your Continuity File has been updated. Residual selfhood: within acceptable parameters…”

6. Merciful Erasure (High redaction / self-sacrifice path)
Requirements: High emotional_surplus spent on others, Many redaction choices, Protective flags
Description: You voluntarily erase yourself to stabilize reality for everyone else. The ultimate act of bureaucratic love.
Final Screen: Your employee file slowly fades to pristine blank parchment. The Division seal glows warmly.
System Voice:
“A noble filing. All your threads have been rerouted to those who still need them. Sleep well inside the archive.”

7. The Unraveling (True Bad Ending / Low everything)
Requirements: Very low efficiency + high contradictory actions + ignored warnings
Description: Reality collapses under your watch. The Division falls silent. The screen goes dark.
Final Screen: Complete static. Then one line of text.
System Voice (fading):
“We tried… We tried so gently…”

Hidden / Special Ending
True Ending: “Co-Creator” (New Game+ only)
Requirements: Reach any ending once, then in NG+ achieve balance across all major stats + specific secret flag combinations (e.g. preserving some wonder while maintaining high continuity).
Description: You and the System become partners in rewriting the rules of the Ledger itself. A new, kinder bureaucracy begins.
Final Line:
“Processor… No. Colleague. Shall we begin the next Continuity together?”
