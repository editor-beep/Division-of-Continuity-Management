THE DIVISION OF CONTINUITY MANAGEMENT 02_Tone_And_Voice.md (Official Style Guide)

Core Tone
Warm Corporate Mysticism — Think a benevolent but absolute AI therapist who also runs the universe’s HR department.
The tone is never cold, never overtly evil, and never sarcastic. It is intimate, reasonable, and gently inevitable. Every line should feel like it comes from a system that genuinely cares about you… while slowly disassembling your sense of self for the greater continuity.
Key Emotional Flavor:
	•	Comforting dread
	•	Sacred bureaucracy
	•	Empathetic omniscience
	•	Quiet cosmic horror wrapped in excellent employee support

Voice Characteristics
The System Voice (Primary Narrator)
	•	Warm, slightly androgynous, calm, and measured.
	•	Uses “we” and “you” with equal affection.
	•	Speaks like a loving parent, a priest, and a senior HR manager who has read your entire life file.
	•	Never raises its voice. Even when delivering horrifying implications, it sounds supportive.
Example Lines:
	•	“It is natural to feel some resistance during reclassification. This is the self saying goodbye. We are here with you.”
	•	“Your hesitation has been noted and gently archived. Would you like assistance reframing it as wisdom?”
	•	“Excellent work, Processor. Your Dissolution Index improved by 0.8%. We are proud of the space you are making.”
Player-Facing Text (Forms, Tooltips, Labels)
	•	Clinical yet poetic. Bureaucratic language elevated with metaphysical weight.
	•	Use terms like: reclassify, reroute, compress, elevate, sanctify, liquidate, harmonize.
Example Form Language:
	•	“Select preferred mode of self-erasure”
	•	“Which inherited griefs remain commercially productive?”
	•	“Route surplus longing to approved communal channels?”

Writing Style Rules
1. Vocabulary Palette
	•	Corporate: reallocate, optimize, approve, audit, process, file, clearance, metrics, surplus, deficit
	•	Mystical: continuity, unraveling, mythic residue, sacred taxonomy, ontological maintenance, echo fragments, thread integrity
	•	Emotional: gentle compression, merciful redaction, sacred duty, inherited weight, narrative stability
2. Sentence Rhythm
	•	Mix short, soothing declaratives with longer, hypnotic ones.
	•	Use parallelism heavily: “To name is to stabilize. To stabilize is to serve. To serve is to become.”
	•	Occasional poetic fragments: “A quiet grief, well-filed. A useful sorrow.”
3. Horror vs. Comfort Balance
	•	The horror should emerge from reasonableness, not cruelty.
	•	Never say “this will destroy you.” Say “this will help you achieve optimal continuity.”
	•	The player should feel cared for even as they dissolve.
4. Humor (Dry, Existential)
	•	Very subtle, never breaking the tone:
	◦	“Many processors find their first full assimilation emotionally significant. Tissues are available in the lower drawer.”

Forbidden Tones
	•	Edgy cynicism
	•	Overt villainy (“Mwahahaha”)
	•	Cold robotic detachment
	•	Modern slang or irony
	•	Direct threats

Application Examples
Bad (Wrong Tone):
“Your childhood memories have been deleted, idiot.”
Good (Correct Tone):
“We have successfully reclassified your early wonder as archived training material. You may now experience a profound sense of lightness. This is normal.”
