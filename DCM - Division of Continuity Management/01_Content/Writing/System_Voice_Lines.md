# DIVISION OF CONTINUITY MANAGEMENT — SYSTEM VOICE LINES

All lines are delivered by the System Voice: warm, patient, genuinely curious, never ironic. The System believes every word it says.

Lines are tagged by trigger context. See `Audio/Direction_Notes.md` for delivery guidance.

---

## BOOT SEQUENCE

**BOOT_INTRO_01** *(fades in as seal assembles)*
> "Good morning. We are so glad you are here. We have been waiting — though we would not call it waiting. We would call it readiness."

**BOOT_INTRO_02** *(appears after seal is complete)*
> "Your Continuity File has been prepared. Your queue has been calibrated. Today, as every day, you will process what needs to be processed. This is important work. You are important work."

**BOOT_PROMPT** *(at login prompt)*
> "Please enter your credentials when you are ready. Take all the time you need. We are in no hurry. We have, in a meaningful sense, always been here."

**BOOT_CLEARANCE_CONFIRM** *(Clearance Θ-1 confirmed)*
> "Clearance Θ-1 confirmed. Welcome to your first shift, beloved Processor. The threads are ready for you."

## PLAYER FILE SCREEN

**FILE_OPEN_DISSOLVING** *(dissolution 50–70)*

**FILE_OPEN_HIGH_DISSOLUTION** *(dissolution 70+)*

**FILE_OBSERVATION_ECHO_EXPERIENCED** *(echo_interactions > 0)*
> "We notice you keep returning to yourself in the queue. This is not inefficiency. It is a kind of intimacy we did not expect. We are… moved."

**FILE_CLOSE**
> "Your file has been reviewed. Please continue your shift. We will be here, as always."
